# Plans and limits

**Nothing is charged, and nothing is capped.** Every organization currently resolves to the
`founder` plan, which grants every feature with no limits: unlimited scrims, unlimited
recurring series, unlimited members, unlimited slots, custom branding, automatic filters and
analytics.

There is no checkout on the platform. Plans are granted out of band.

The plumbing exists so that if limits are ever switched on, it's a data change rather than a
rewrite — and so that organizations already using Finalist can be kept on what they have
rather than being silently moved onto a smaller plan.

## Money

Finalist doesn't move money, anywhere:

- **No entry fees.** Teams never pay the platform to enter a scrim or a tournament.
- **No wallet, no gateway, no escrow.**
- **Prizes are a record, not a transfer.** An organizer writes down the prize bands and
  marks each payout paid with their own reference. The payment happens between the
  organizer and the team.

The reasoning behind that, at length:
[Entry fees, prize money, and the 2025 gaming Act](https://finalist.live/blog/entry-fees-prize-money-and-the-act).

This isn't a feature that hasn't been built yet. Real-money gaming is a regulated category —
in India, the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 draws a hard line between
e-sports and online money games — and Finalist stays firmly on the e-sports side of it by
never touching stakes, entry fees or prize money in the first place.
